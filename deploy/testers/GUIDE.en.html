Movies-Cast {{VERSION}} Guide for testers

Play the films of IceWhale Movies on a DLNA TV, controlled from your phone. The TV plays the original file itself; only if it stays silent is the sound converted, on request.

Thank you for testing! This version is a test build. Please report anything that fails or surprises you, using the template at the end. Your report goes to the person who gave you this package.

0. How it works – no app on the TV

  • Movies-Cast is a second app on your ZimaOS, next to Movies. You install it from the file docker-compose.yml in this package (section 2).
  • Your phone is the remote. You open the Movies-Cast page in the phone's browser, pick a film and a TV, and press play. Pause, jump and stop are on the phone too.
  • Nothing is installed on the TV. The TV only has to understand DLNA ("Media Renderer"); Movies-Cast sends it the film, and the TV plays it itself.
  • The TV app shown in the video (LG webOS, pairing code, library on the TV) is not part of this package. For Android TV (e.g. Nvidia Shield) there is a test app, see section 9.
  • Is my TV or box suitable? Install Movies-Cast and switch the device on: if it appears in the TV list on the phone, it answers as a DLNA video renderer. If not, the text below the list says what the search found.

1. Requirements

  • ZimaOS on a machine with an x86-64 processor (ZimaBoard, ZimaBlade, ZimaCube, PC). ARM devices do not work with this package.
  • IceWhale's Movies app installed on the same ZimaOS, with at least one library of films. Movies-Cast logs you in to Movies with your ZimaOS account. Movies is a beta for now (module zimaos-movies, version 0.3.0 or later; Movies-Cast runs with 0.4.0, measured 2026-10-29) and is neither in the App Store nor in ZimaOS' module list. If you do not have Movies, contact the person who gave you this package.
  • A TV with DLNA (often called "Media Renderer", "DLNA" or "media sharing") on the same network as ZimaOS.
  • A phone or computer with a browser on the same network.
  • Internet on the ZimaOS machine for the installation: the program is downloaded from {{IMAGE}}.

2. Install

  1. In the ZimaOS dashboard, click "+" next to Apps, then the entry for installing a custom app. The same button is also on the start page of the App Store.
  2. At the top of the dialog choose "Import" → "Docker Compose" and select the file docker-compose.yml from this package. ZimaOS confirms the import and shows the logo, the image ghcr.io/chicohaager/movies-cast and port 8097.
  3. Click the install button at the bottom ("Installieren" in the German interface). ZimaOS downloads the program (about 9 MB) and adds the tile "Movies-Cast" with the Movies-Cast logo.
  4. If the ZFW firewall runs, it shows two "new app port" cards (8097/tcp and 1901/udp, "from your LAN only"). LAN only is enough for Movies-Cast.

The app keeps its data in /DATA/AppData/movies-cast/state (logins, TV names, proven formats, your settings).

3. First steps

  1. Open the tile – or on your phone http://<address-of-your-ZimaOS>:8097. Tip: bookmark it or "Add to Home Screen".
  2. Log in with your ZimaOS account.
  3. Pick the TV at the top. The TV has to be switched on.
  4. Tap a film → "Play from the start" (or "Continue at …").
  5. In the footer you can choose the language and the appearance (System, Light, Dark); both apply to your account on every device.
No TV in the list?
  • The text below the list says why: no answer to the search, answers without a video device, or all hidden.
  • Under "Manage TVs" you can add the TV by its IP address.
  • If ZimaOS runs a firewall, it has to allow UDP port 1901 from the home network (the TVs' answers to the search).
  • The "Status" card shows whether Movies is reachable, when the last search ran and what it found.
What else you may see
  • Format hint: if the TV does not list the film's format, Movies-Cast asks first. "Play anyway" tries it – many TVs play more than they list.
  • "No sound?" in the player restarts the film at the same place with converted sound. This is remembered per TV and audio format; "Original sound" undoes it.
  • "– off" after a TV's name: it was seen before but does not answer now. "Switch on and play" tries to wake it by Wake-on-LAN (an LG needs "Mobile TV On" enabled).

6. Update to a new version

An update needs no uninstall and no new import. The labels below are those of the German interface, where this was measured:

  1. In the ZimaOS dashboard open the tile's "…" menu and choose "Verwalten" (manage).
  2. In the field "Tag" enter the new version number, for this package {{VERSION}}.
  3. If the field "App-Titel" (app title) still holds the old name "Movies auf dem Fernseher" or "Movies Cast" (up to 0.24.0), enter "Movies-Cast" – otherwise the tile keeps its old name.
  4. Click "Speichern" (save). ZimaOS downloads the new program ("Aktualisieren … %") and restarts Movies-Cast.

Saving writes what the fields hold – if you later open "Verwalten" for something else, check before saving that "Tag" still shows the running version. Logins and settings are kept. The "Status" card then shows the new version. Measured on ZimaOS 1.8.0, updating from 0.24.0 to 0.25.0.

7. Remove

Uninstall the tile in the ZimaOS dashboard. Note: ZimaOS then also deletes /DATA/AppData/movies-cast – all logins and settings of Movies-Cast. Your films and Movies itself are not touched.

8. Known limits of this version

  • The TV app for LG webOS is not part of this package; this test is about the DLNA path.
  • The translations other than German and English were made by an AI and have not been read by a native speaker yet – corrections are welcome.
  • Playback has been checked with ZimaOS 1.8.0 and an LG OLED55B9. A Samsung UE50MU6179 was found; whether it plays is still open – reports about other TVs are especially valuable.
  • Updating from 0.24.0 or 0.25.0: an update via "Tag" brings only the new program, no change to the compose file. Movies-Cast then writes its log in UTC instead of local time – this is harmless. To change it: in "Verwalten" open "YAML", delete the line - TZ=$TZ, add the /etc/localtime entry under volumes: as in this package's docker-compose.yml, and save (measured on ZimaOS 1.8.0; logins are kept).

9. Android TV app (test build)

For Android TV devices such as the Nvidia Shield: the file movies-cast-tv-{{VERSION}}.apk from the release page (https://github.com/chicohaager/movies-cast/releases). It shows the library on the TV and is operated with the TV remote, like the LG app in the video. It needs Movies-Cast {{VERSION}} on ZimaOS.

  1. Install the APK on the TV device. One way: on the device enable the developer options and network debugging, then on a computer adb connect <address-of-the-device> and adb install movies-cast-tv-{{VERSION}}.apk.
  2. Open "Movies-Cast" on the device and enter the address of your ZimaOS (the address your browser shows for the ZimaOS dashboard, without "http://"; port 8097 is added).
  3. The TV shows a six-digit code. On the phone, in Movies-Cast, open "Pair the TV app" and enter the code (or scan the QR code).
  4. Remote: arrows and OK to choose; in the film OK = pause, ◀ −10 s, ▶ +30 s, ▲ audio track, ▼ subtitles; Back = end.

Please check the sound: in the Android emulator, films with AC3 or E-AC3 sound played without sound (AAC worked). Whether a Shield plays them with sound is not known yet. Please try one film whose line below the title says AC3 or E-AC3 and one with AAC, and report "sound yes/no" for each.
